1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Trio Chef Studio handles information in the current release of the Trio Chef mobile game and on this website. It applies only to Trio Chef and this official privacy and support website.

2. Information We Collect

Trio Chef Studio does not collect or receive personal information through the current release of the game. The game does not require an account, connect to a custom backend, or transmit gameplay data to Trio Chef Studio.

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. It does not collect advertising identifiers or payment information.

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising technology, contact forms, or other tracking tools. If you contact support by email, your email provider and ours will process your message and email address so that we can respond.

3. Local Game Data

Trio Chef stores information needed to run the game locally on your device. This includes:

  • level progress, stars, and best scores;
  • earned Chef Coins and Chef Tokens, which are virtual gameplay items with no cash value;
  • owned and selected cosmetic card styles;
  • sound, haptic-feedback, and reduced-motion settings;
  • tutorial completion; and
  • aggregate play counters, such as level starts, wins, losses, retries, and feature use.

These aggregate counters contain no player identifier and remain on the device. Local game data is not sent to Trio Chef Studio or to a third-party analytics service.

4. Third-Party Services

The current release does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, remote crash-reporting, authentication, cloud-storage, or social-network services. Sound and haptic feedback are processed on the device.

When you download or update Trio Chef, the app store and operating-system provider may process information under their own privacy policies. Your device's backup service may also include locally stored app data, depending on your device and backup settings. Trio Chef Studio does not control this independent processing.

5. Advertising and In-App Purchases

The current public release contains no real advertising and no in-app purchases. Chef Coins and Chef Tokens are earned and used only through gameplay; they cannot be bought with money and have no cash value.

Internal development builds may use a mock rewarded-ad control for testing. It does not connect to an advertising network and is not available in the public release.

If advertising, purchases, or another third-party service is added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when that service is introduced.

6. Younger Players

Trio Chef is designed to be suitable for a broad audience. The current release does not request a player's name, age, contact details, or account information. Parents and guardians should supervise younger players' device and email use as appropriate.

7. Data Retention and Security

Settings → Reset Progress returns progress, scores, virtual currencies, cosmetics, tutorials, and preferences to their defaults. It does not clear the separate aggregate play counters. To remove all locally stored Trio Chef data, you can clear the app's storage through your device settings where that option is available, or uninstall the game. A copy may remain in a device backup until that backup is deleted or expires under your backup provider's practices.

Because the game does not send this local data to Trio Chef Studio, we cannot access, restore, or remotely delete it.

We design Trio Chef to minimize data use. No method of device storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security of information stored on your device.

If you email support, the message may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, and meet legal obligations. You may ask us to delete a support conversation, subject to any required retention.

8. International Users

The game does not transfer local game data to Trio Chef Studio. If you contact us by email from another country, your message may be processed in countries where email service providers operate and where privacy laws may differ from those in your country.

9. Your Choices

You can turn sound effects and haptic feedback off in the game settings. You can reset player progress in the game. To remove all locally stored Trio Chef data, including aggregate play counters, use your device's app-storage controls where available or uninstall Trio Chef. You can manage device backups through your operating-system or backup-provider settings.

You do not need to contact support to play the game. If you have sent us an email and want to ask about that correspondence, use the contact details below.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Trio Chef changes or when required for legal or store-compliance reasons. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the date at the top. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel when required.
