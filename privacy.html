Trio Chef Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Trio Chef is a casual memory game. It does not require a Trio Chef account, and core gameplay works offline. Advertising, consent, purchases, store restoration, support links, and optional sharing can use network or external platform services.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Trio Chef Studio handles information in the current release of the Trio Chef mobile game and on this website. It applies only to Trio Chef and this official privacy and support website.

2. Information We Collect

The game does not require a Trio Chef account, and Trio Chef Studio does not operate a gameplay backend. Gameplay progress and anonymous playtest counters are stored locally. Advertising and purchase providers may process information as described below.

The game does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location. Advertising providers may process identifiers and approximate location derived from your IP address. Payment details are handled by Google Play and are not received or stored by Trio Chef Studio.

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising technology, contact forms, or other tracking tools. If you contact support by email, your email provider and ours will process your message and email address so that we can respond.

3. Local Game Data

Trio Chef stores information needed to run the game locally on your device. This includes:

  • level progress, stars, and best scores;
  • Chef Coins and Chef Tokens;
  • delivered purchase transaction identifiers and the Remove Ads entitlement;
  • owned and selected cosmetic card styles;
  • sound, haptic-feedback, and reduced-motion settings;
  • tutorial completion; and
  • anonymous playtest counters, such as level starts, wins, losses, retries, and feature use.

This data is used to run the game, prevent duplicate purchase delivery, and restore progress. It is not automatically sent to Trio Chef Studio or to a separate remote gameplay analytics service.

The Beta Report screen can prepare a plain-text summary of local playtest counters. Nothing is sent automatically. You explicitly choose whether, where, and to whom the report is shared through your device's share sheet. The report does not intentionally include a name, email address, advertising ID, or device identifier.

4. Third-Party Services

Android builds integrate AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent, and Google Play and RevenueCat for in-app purchases when billing is enabled. The current testing status and the information these providers may process are described below.

The game does not currently use a separate remote gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service. Sound and haptic feedback are processed on the device.

When you download or update Trio Chef, the app store and operating-system provider may process information under their own privacy policies. Your device's backup service may also include locally stored app data, depending on your device and backup settings. Trio Chef Studio does not control this independent processing.

5. Advertising and In-App Purchases

Advertising and purchase integrations are currently being tested. Current testing builds use Google's official test ad units, and purchases default to a simulation that does not charge money. Google Play test purchases may be used when billing is enabled for testing. These integrations do not mean that live advertising or real-money purchases are currently active in the public release.

Android builds use AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded videos and occasional interstitials. Google's User Messaging Platform requests or records advertising privacy choices where required. Ads are requested only when Google's consent system reports that ads may be requested. The Settings screen offers a privacy-options entry point whenever Google reports that it is required. You can use that form to review or change your advertising choices and withdraw consent where applicable.

Google and its advertising partners may process data such as device or other identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices to deliver, measure, secure, limit, or personalize ads, depending on your region and choices.

When available, the Remove Ads purchase disables automatic interstitials; optional rewarded videos remain available when you select them. See Google's privacy information and Google's advertising controls.

When billing is enabled, Android in-app purchases are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. Products include consumable Chef Token packs and a non-consumable Remove Ads purchase. The game receives product, transaction, purchase-status, and entitlement data needed to display prices, deliver each purchase once, restore Remove Ads, and handle errors or pending transactions.

The app disables RevenueCat automatic device-identifier collection and diagnostics where supported, but RevenueCat and Google Play still process the technical and transaction information necessary to operate purchases. Payment details are handled by Google Play and are not received or stored by Trio Chef Studio.

See Google Play's privacy information and RevenueCat's privacy policy.

6. Younger Players

The current release is intended for a general audience aged 16 and over and is not specifically directed to children. Parents or guardians should supervise purchases and device use. If Trio Chef Studio later targets children, the app, advertising configuration, store declarations, and this policy must be reviewed before that release.

7. Data Retention and Security

Settings → Reset Progress deletes gameplay progression but preserves Chef Tokens, delivered-purchase records, and the Remove Ads entitlement. It does not clear the separate playtest counters. To remove ordinary locally stored Trio Chef data, you can clear the app's storage through your device settings where that option is available, or uninstall the game. A copy may remain in a device backup until that backup is deleted or expires under your backup provider's practices.

Purchases associated with your Google Play account may remain available through Google Play and RevenueCat after you uninstall the app. Google Play purchase and order data may need to be managed through your Google account or a support request.

Trio Chef Studio cannot access, restore, or remotely delete gameplay data stored only on your device. Advertising and purchase providers handle the information they process under their own privacy policies.

We design Trio Chef to minimize data use. No method of device storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security of information stored on your device.

If you email support, the message may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, and meet legal obligations. You may ask us to delete a support conversation, subject to any required retention.

8. International Users

The game does not automatically transfer local gameplay progress or playtest counters to Trio Chef Studio. Advertising, consent, purchase, and sharing services may process information in countries where their providers operate, under their own privacy policies. If you contact us by email from another country, your message may be processed in countries where email service providers operate and where privacy laws may differ from those in your country.

9. Your Choices

You can decline optional rewarded videos and, when available, buy Remove Ads to disable automatic interstitials. You can manage advertising choices and withdraw consent where applicable through Google's privacy-options form in Settings whenever that entry point is available.

You can turn sound effects and haptic feedback off in the game settings and choose whether to share a Beta Report. You can reset gameplay progress in Settings, subject to the preserved data described above. To remove ordinary locally stored Trio Chef data, including playtest counters, use your device's app-storage controls where available or uninstall Trio Chef. You can manage device backups through your operating-system or backup-provider settings. Google Play purchase and order data may need to be managed through your Google account or a support request.

You do not need to contact support to play the game. If you have sent us an email and want to ask about that correspondence, use the contact details below.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Trio Chef changes or when required for legal or store-compliance reasons. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the date at the top. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel when required.
